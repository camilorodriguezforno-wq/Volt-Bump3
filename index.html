How SpeedBank Works

A concept for harvesting a small amount of energy from road traffic.

01

Vehicle Pressure

A vehicle passes over a specially engineered road module.

02

Mechanical Motion

A moving mechanism transfers part of the applied force.

03

Electricity

A generator converts mechanical motion into electrical energy.

04

Storage

Suitable electronics manage and store the harvested energy.

Possible Applications

Low-power LEDs, road sensors, counters and educational monitoring systems.

Engineering challenge: the system must be safe, durable, weather-resistant and efficient. Road traffic safety and vehicle comfort come first.

Our Mission

SpeedBank explores how everyday movement could contribute to small-scale energy generation. We want to encourage innovation, sustainability and smarter infrastructure.

Our Vision

To investigate practical ways of recovering otherwise unused mechanical energy and applying it to useful low-power devices.

Why SpeedBank?

Our project connects science, engineering, environmental awareness and business thinking in one demonstrable concept.

Our next milestone: build a safe prototype, measure its electrical output and compare results with our simulations.
