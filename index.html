THE TECHNOLOGY

How it works

Recovering a portion of mechanical motion requires a carefully engineered system.

01

Vehicle pressure

A wheel applies a load to a specially engineered movable module designed for safe passage.

02

Mechanical transfer

A guided mechanism transfers some of the movement to a generator. Springs and gearing are possible design elements.

03

Electrical conversion

A generator converts mechanical input into electrical output. Voltage and current must be measured.

04

Power conditioning

Suitable electronics regulate the output to protect connected components and manage varying voltage.

05

Energy storage

A properly designed storage system can collect energy for later low-power use.

06

Monitoring

Sensors can record voltage, current, energy, temperature and system performance.

What should we measure?

To turn this concept into an evidence-based project, measure the prototype's voltage and current under repeatable conditions, and integrate power over time to estimate electrical energy.

VOLTAGE
V

Electrical potential difference.

CURRENT
A

Electric charge flow per second.

POWER
W

Instantaneous electrical power: P = V × I for DC.

ENERGY
Wh

Power accumulated over a period of time.

A real roadway product would require professional structural, electrical, accessibility and road-safety testing. This school concept is not a certified road device.
